Up a Creek ↗

David Schurman Wallace

A meditation on John McPhee’s patient, expansive nature writing—and on what disappears when readers and writers no longer linger with a river, forest, glacier, or stretch of tundra. Moving through the Colorado River, Lake Powell, the Pine Barrens, and Alaska, Wallace argues that sustained attention is itself a form of ecological care.

The Incredible Journey of a Migratory Shorebird ↗

Rivka Galchen

From Chilean Patagonia to Alaskan nesting bogs, Galchen follows Hudsonian godwits on a nearly ten-thousand-mile migration. Field travel and vivid natural history mingle with Māori legend, old beliefs that birds flew to the moon, and the almost fabulist fact that godwits shrink and regrow organs for flight.
