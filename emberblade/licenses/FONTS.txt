Emberblade embeds two typefaces, both licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(https://openfontlicense.org):

- Cinzel, by Natanael Gama (https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Cinzel)
- Nunito, by Vernon Adams and contributors (https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Nunito)

The fonts are embedded unmodified (Latin subset) as base64 data in index.html.
The full license text is at https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/
